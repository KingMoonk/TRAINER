WHY THE REST OF THE APP SAYS BITS PER CHARACTER
Nats per piece depend on the word list: bigger pieces cost more nats each. Dividing by characters makes versions with different word lists comparable.
bits per character = nats per piece ÷ (characters per piece × ln 2)
This piece, “ Jupiter”, is 8 characters with its space: {{ loss }} ÷ (8 × 0.693) = {{ bpc }} bits per character. Over a whole text the engine uses the totals: all nats ÷ (all characters × ln 2).
