THE VERDICT {{ verdict.line }} {{ verdict.sub }}
Training saved. Take the newer version’s error on held-back text. Find how much training the older one needed to reach that same error. The gap is the saving. Read off the two error-versus-compute curves; no new runs. Only runs trained to the same length on the same schedule are compared; otherwise there is no verdict. Checked at several sizes and seeds. A change only counts if the saving holds at two or three sizes and is larger than the spread across seeds. Gains that appear at one size and vanish at another are treated as noise.
