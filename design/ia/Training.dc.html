How the status and schedule work
Status is one of three, decided from the run: Learning (smoothed error fell by more than the step-to-step noise over the last window), Flat (it didn’t), Memorising (held-back error rising while training error falls). Repeated passes over the same text help less each time and make memorising likely after a few; the fix is more text, or stopping at the best checkpoint. Schedule: warm-up, then a long steady stretch, then a cool-down where the learning rate falls to near zero. Checkpoints taken mid-steady look worse than they are; “Stop after a short cool-down” and “Make a checkpoint now” (in Queue and schedule) cool down a copy first. Learning rate “auto”: version 4’s tuned rate, rescaled for the new width so it doesn’t need retuning (μP). Change it only in Advanced.
