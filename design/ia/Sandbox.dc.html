WHAT EACH WRITES version 4 [sample] your idea [sample]
Fair by time, not steps. Both get the same minutes and the same tiny dataset, read in the same order. A slower design gets fewer steps, which is the honest answer to “what do I get for my time?”. Shrunk to a quarter. Both designs keep their shape at a quarter of the width, so a test fits in minutes. The learning rate is carried over with μP, so neither is starved by the smaller width. Small-size rankings can still flip at full size: treat a shrunk result as a hint. Memorize one batch. The idea trains on a single small batch over and over. A working model drives its error on that batch close to zero; if it can’t, something in the design or its maths is broken. Starts where it should. An untrained model guesses almost uniformly, so its first error should sit near the uniform-guess line (the log of the word-list size). Far above that points to a bad starting setup.
